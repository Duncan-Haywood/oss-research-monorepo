Follow-up: reputation-weighted mechanisms (closes README Limitations 1-3)
=========================================================================
Bootstraps a per-verifier trust weight from Peer Truth Serum payoffs
measured on a held-out calibration window (the first 30% of tasks), then uses it to
(a) re-estimate the Correlated Agreement delta matrix from
trust-weighted pairs and discount each payment by the payee's own
weight, and (b) scale LMSR trade size -- all measured only on the
disjoint scoring window, so nothing here reuses reports it used to
estimate trust with.
