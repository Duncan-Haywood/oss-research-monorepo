# oss-monorepo
Duncan's open source software monorepo (MIT licensed). Portfolio page: `docs/index.html` (GitHub Pages, serve from `/docs`).

- [`research/decentralized-verification-markets`](research/decentralized-verification-markets) — peer-prediction and prediction-market mechanisms for verifying decentralized ML training, with adversarial evaluation.
- [`research/market-routing`](research/market-routing) — cost-function markets as expert routers: LMSR = Hedge, quadratic potential = sparsemax; regret/loss bounds verified, continual-learning simulation.
- [`research/wagering-modular-experts`](research/wagering-modular-experts) — self-financed wagering mechanisms as verifiable routers for modular, continually learning experts; shows plain WSWM locks in under recurring regimes and a fixed-share tax fixes it at the cost of subsidising free-riders.
- [`research/property-elicitation-verification`](research/property-elicitation-verification) — property elicitation (quantile/expectile/Bregman scores, non-elicitability of variance) applied to pricing tail-drift tolerance thresholds in verifiable ML training.
- [`research/verification-game`](research/verification-game) — inspection-game economics of refereed verification: stake substitutes for re-execution, extra verifiers free-ride, and optimal tolerance/stake under floating-point drift.
- [`research/verifier-bribery`](research/verifier-bribery) — bribing verifiers in refereed verification: closed-form bribe floor, a sharp collusion-proof stake that falls as 1/m, and jackpots that raise the floor one-for-one.
- [`research/reproducible-refereed-training`](research/reproducible-refereed-training) — measured float32 reduction-order drift, RepOps-style canonical order, Merkle-committed training traces with a teacher-forced referee; per-step tolerance is a cumulative hidden-deviation budget.
- [`research/private-markets`](research/private-markets) — differentially private LMSR via the binary-tree mechanism: path-wise loss bound, and the accuracy/subsidy cost of privacy.
- [`research/audit-dynamics`](research/audit-dynamics) — no-regret learning in the refereed-verification inspection game: conserved potential, closed-form cycle period, averages converge but per-round audit probability collapses; optimistic Hedge converges.
- [`research/multi-verifier-audit`](research/multi-verifier-audit) — m verifiers in the inspection game: detection pinned at s/(s+S), cheating rises with m under split rewards, symmetric equilibrium is a saddle (volunteer's dilemma), bandit learners need an exploration floor.
- [`research/holdout-market`](research/holdout-market) — paying for model improvements against a hidden holdout: telescoping budget, a payment-feedback overfitting attack (paid 0.15 while true loss worsens 0.37), and the Ladder fix with its measured cost.
- [`research/spot-check-slashing`](research/spot-check-slashing) — sampled audits of committed training traces: payoff is convex in corrupted steps, deterrence iff `min(F, f·k) ≥ T·g`, so stake and audits are complements and stake must scale with job length.
- [`research/quantized-reports`](research/quantized-reports) — finite-precision probability reports under proper scoring: optimal-grid regret law matched to 0.3%, uniform grids lose a `ln N` factor under log score, raw minifloat formats are ~13× worse than symmetrised.
- [`research/effort-elicitation`](research/effort-elicitation) — paying verifiers for costly checking effort with proper scoring rules: implementable efforts = concave envelope of the value curve, exact Brier rent `(n−1)/(a+b)`, log score needs half Brier's payout spread on rare-fault priors.
- [`research/expert-pooling`](research/expert-pooling) — pooling calibrated experts with shared training data: Bayes-optimal extremization `a* = n/(1+(n−1)ρ)` verified numerically, saturation at `n_eff → 1/ρ`, asymmetric robustness, and online learning of the exponent.
- [`research/effort-contracts`](research/effort-contracts) — scoring rules as limited-liability effort contracts: shirking threshold `2c/(kκ²)`, first best needs a fee, otherwise the principal keeps 48–68% of surplus.
- [`research/peer-prediction-effort`](research/peer-prediction-effort) — peer prediction without ground truth: output agreement pays 1.0 for laziness vs 0.68 for honesty, Dasgupta–Ghosh payoff `2p(1-p)g_i g_j` exact, effort as a coordination game with threshold `c/(2p(1-p)κ²)`, and closed-form gold-check rate `√(c·g_H/G)`.
- [`research/decision-regret-transfer`](research/decision-regret-transfer) — from a verifier's excess proper score to a certified cap on accept/audit/slash decision regret: exact calibration functions (Brier ε², log KL), linear transfer only for non-proper hinge, audit action tightens the guarantee.
- [`research/reject-surrogates`](research/reject-surrogates) — polyhedral surrogates for accept/reject/escalate verifiers: minimiser embeds the Bayes action, linear regret transfer with constant exactly `2d`, logistic transfer ratio `2d(1-d)/δ`, and a negative SGD result.
- [`research/sybil-stake`](research/sybil-stake) — identity splitting vs merging under stake-weighted pools: split-invariant iff the weight is linear in stake, per-head optimum `√(Rn/c)−n`, sharp deterrence fee `Rn/((n+1)(n+2))`, wagering split-neutral.
- [`research/robust-aggregation`](research/robust-aggregation) — Byzantine-robust aggregation of verifier reports: mean bias `βB` unbounded, median bias `σΦ⁻¹(1/(2(1−β)))` independent of B, trimmed-mean closed form for τ≥β verified by simulation.
- [`research/sequential-slashing`](research/sequential-slashing) — anytime-valid slashing of miscalibrated verifiers: peeking z-test falsely slashes 37% of honest verifiers (nominal 5%), an e-process holds ≤α by Ville, detection delay ≈ `ln(1/α)/KL` (within 12%), q-free mixture costs ×1.36.
- [`research/gossip-consensus`](research/gossip-consensus) — pairwise gossip for decentralised training: exact contraction `(n−2)/(2(n−1))≈½` per matching round (~20 rounds at any n), noise floor `(n−1)(n−2)s²/n`, one stubborn node captures consensus at rate `1−1/(n(n−1))`, trust-region clipping caps drift at `τ/(n(n−1))` per step.
- [`research/verifier-complements`](research/verifier-complements) — verifiers as informational complements: decision value is zero below a closed-form committee size while mutual information is submodular; myopic hiring buys nothing and leave-one-out pay is a sawtooth that vanishes.
- [`research/dispute-arity`](research/dispute-arity) — k-ary bisection dispute games: optimal arity solves `k ln k − k + 1 = a/b` (binary only if `a/b ≤ 0.386`), 1.9–6× cheaper than binary at T=10⁶, adaptive schedules gain ≤1%, leaf-size trade-off, latency as extra round cost.
- [`research/compute-procurement`](research/compute-procurement) — buying k units of decentralised compute by reverse auction: exact VCG payment `k(k+1)/(n+1)` is exactly 2× winners' cost, optimal reserve is exactly `v/2`, first-price equilibrium matches by revenue equivalence.
- [`research/delayed-routing`](research/delayed-routing) — Hedge/LMSR routing with delayed feedback: regret `≤ ln N/η + ηT/8 + ηD/2` (mean delay inflates horizon by `1+4d̄`), 12× regret from ignoring delay against a reactive adversary, delay-tuned rate too cautious on oblivious data, LMSR subsidy ~20× at mean delay 5.
- [`research/replicated-execution`](research/replicated-execution) — random k-fold replication with slashing against a colluding fraction β: secret assignment makes cheating a coordination game with unique-honesty stake `Gβ^{k−1}/(1−β^{k−1})`, public assignment leaves `β^k` corruption at any stake, and assignment leaks leave a `λβ^k` floor.
- [`research/market-manipulation`](research/market-manipulation) — manipulating an LMSR verification market: manipulator loss `b·KL(q‖τ)` equals the extra informed rent, and manipulation raises accuracy only in a liquidity band (thin: costs `1−2q`; deep: neutral).
- [`research/audit-allocation`](research/audit-allocation) — scarce audit budget across heterogeneous jobs: deterrence threshold `g/(g+S)` makes it a knapsack, greedy with a 1/3 guarantee (within 1% on 91% of instances), uniform audits need up to 1.9× the budget, heterogeneity lowers deterrence cost (Jensen), stake substitutes for audits.
- [`research/churn-checkpointing`](research/churn-checkpointing) — checkpoint interval for preemptible decentralised training: exact Lambert-W optimum independent of restart cost, Young/Daly overshoot 5–25%, flat within 2×, synchronous efficiency wall (50% at n≈691), flaky workers cut throughput 5×.
- [`research/data-valuation-replication`](research/data-valuation-replication) — Shapley and leave-one-out payments under replication: Shapley pays every extra copy even when worthless, saturating at stand-alone value (`(n+2)/2`× honest), naive evaluators hand over the pot, LOO pays copies 0 but distributes 1/(n+1), dedup underpays lookalikes by 45%.
- [`research/escalation-bonds`](research/escalation-bonds) — escalating-bond dispute games: exact deterrence opening bond `εV/((1−ε)C−εD)` shrinks geometrically in rounds while the attacker's total outlay barely moves, and a sharp capital race where the defender needs `γ·max{C(r) ≤ B_C}` ≤ `γ·B_C`.
- [`research/local-sgd-bias`](research/local-sgd-bias) — local-SGD/DiLoCo-style averaging on heterogeneous quadratics: exact fixed point with saturating weights `1-(1-ηa_i)^H`, non-monotone bias to the unweighted mean, exact `a_i/w_i` correction, step-count inflation moves the fixed point 0.50→0.91, wall-clock-optimal sync interval.
- [`research/inference-substitution`](research/inference-substitution) — detecting a provider that serves a cheaper model on a fraction of queries: mixture e-process (valid under peeking), delay `∝ 1/(f p² χ²)`, so the cheater's best response is to leak rarely and steal `∝ √N` not `N`; closed-form audit rate and cost-optimal check rate `(hK/2Nc)^{2/3}`.
- [`research/contest-precision`](research/contest-precision) — winner-take-all training contests scored on a noisy holdout: effort `V a_n √m/s` with `a_2 = a_3 = 1/(2√π)` exactly, a precision ceiling `V/σ² ≤ λ_max(n)` beyond which no pure equilibrium exists (below the zero-rent limit), and the resulting optimal holdout size.
- [`research/risk-averse-scoring`](research/risk-averse-scoring) — risk-averse verifiers under proper scoring: log score reports exactly `odds^(1/(1+αb))`, Brier reports solve `logit p = logit r + k(2r−1)` and can be debiased exactly, a mean-`k` aggregation correction is worse than none, and a binarised lottery rule is truthful for any utility at a 2.6–27% certainty-equivalent cost.
- [`research/fiat-shamir-grinding`](research/fiat-shamir-grinding) — hash-derived audit challenges under free retries: cheat profit exactly `G − c/p`, deterrence needs `ln(G/c)·T/k` opened steps vs `ln2·T/k` for a beacon (10×–60×), stake is irrelevant, and the proof-of-work optimum is `x* = v/λ_p − c0`.
- [`research/anytime-liquidity`](research/anytime-liquidity) — LMSR with time-varying liquidity as anytime Hedge: `b_t = c*√t` costs exactly `√2` over the horizon-aware bound, doubling is 1.70× worse, and self-tuning liquidity makes the subsidy equal the accumulated mixability gap (1.3 vs 102 on easy data).
- [`research/abstention-scoring`](research/abstention-scoring) — selective reporting under proper scoring: abstention iff `|p−α| < √c` (Brier), silence is evidence unless the anchor is a symmetric prior, ignoring it costs 30× the log-loss at n=12, and a stale anchor makes silence point at the wrong state.
