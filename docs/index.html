Research portfolio

What does infrequent averaging do to the answer?

Local-SGD / DiLoCo-style averaging of heterogeneous quadratic workers has a closed-form fixed point: a weighted mean of the workers' optima with weights 1−(1−ηai)H that saturate, so it is exactly unbiased at H=1, forgets curvature as H grows (converging to the unweighted mean), and is non-monotone in between. Weighting by ai/wi removes the bias exactly; if the server trusts claimed step counts, a low-curvature worker moves the fixed point from 0.50 to 0.91 by claiming 100 steps instead of 5, tying communication savings to verified work.
