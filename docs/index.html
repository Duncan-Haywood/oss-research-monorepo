Research portfolio

Open-source (MIT) research code and write-ups on incentive-compatible verification of decentralized ML training, building on algorithmic economics and information elicitation (CU Boulder, Frongillo, Waggoner) and Gensyn's verifiable-ML work.

Peer-prediction markets for decentralized ML training verification

Proper scoring rules, Peer Truth Serum, Correlated Agreement and LMSR aggregation, with eight follow-ups on mechanism-aware adversaries (trust farming, whitewashing, closed-loop attackers) and defences. Pure-Python, dependency-free, 72 tests.

Markets as routers: LMSR = Hedge, quadratic potential = sparsemax

Cost-function markets as online learners for modular, continual-learning expert routing: verified equivalence and loss/regret bounds, and a switching-environment simulation where the quadratic market stays sparse and low-regret at liquidity levels where LMSR degrades. Small-scale; limitations stated.

Wagering mechanisms as routers for modular, continually learning experts

Weighted-score wagering (Lambert et al.) as a budget-balanced, replayable mixture-of-experts gate under recurring regime drift: plain wagering locks in, fixed-share wealth tax repairs it but subsidises free-riders. Pure-Python, 13 tests.

Closed-form bribe floor for buying verifier silence in refereed verification: a sharp collusion-proof stake (falls as 1/m verifiers), and planted-fault jackpots that lift the floor. Verified against brute force and Monte Carlo. Pure-Python, 7 tests, stylised model.

Reproducible operators and refereed training

Measured float32 drift across reduction orders (relative drift is Cauchy-tailed, state-level drift is a few ulps), a Merkle-committed training trace with a teacher-forced referee, and the finding that a per-step tolerance is a cumulative hidden-deviation budget while bitwise-reproducible operators remove it. Pure-Python, 10 tests.

The subsidy cost of privacy in prediction markets

Differentially private LMSR via the binary-tree mechanism: error scales as 1/(bε), tree noise beats per-release noise ~15×, and privacy noise is exploitable so market-maker loss far exceeds b ln 2. Pure-Python, 6 tests, stylised.

Closed-form m-verifier audit equilibrium (detection pinned at s/(s+S), redundancy bounded by ln(1/(1-q))/q, cheating rising with m under split rewards), a saddle-point stability result, Hedge dynamics that concentrate audit burden on a subset, and bandit learners that need an exploration floor. Paying for improvements, not for overfitting

A model-improvement market paying holdout-loss decreases: the budget telescopes, but naive payment feedback lets a data-less adversary be paid while the published model's true loss worsens; a Ladder-style threshold removes the attack at a measured cost to honest small contributors. Pure-Python, 4 tests, stylised.

Are stake and audits substitutes in trace verification?

Sampled audits of a committed training trace under capped slashing: cheating payoff is convex in the number of corrupted steps, and deterrence holds iff min(F, f·k) ≥ T·g. Stake and audits are complements, so stake must scale with job length. Exact hypergeometric computation, 7 tests, stylised.

Effort elicitation with proper scoring rules in an exact beta-binomial model: implementable efforts are the concave envelope of the value curve, Brier information rent is exactly (n−1)/(a+b) times effort cost, and the log score needs half Brier's payout spread on rare-fault priors. How much should a router extremize?

Pooling calibrated experts with shared training data: the Bayes-optimal logit exponent a* = n/(1+(n−1)ρ) (matched to a numerical argmin), extra experts saturating at 1/ρ effective experts, naive independence pooling doing worse than chance at ρ=0.9, and an online learner that finds a*. Peer prediction for unverifiable labelling and judging work: output agreement pays 1.0 for always answering the majority label versus 0.68 for honesty, the Dasgupta–Ghosh payoff is exactly 2p(1−p)gigj, effort becomes a coordination game with a closed-form threshold (12.8× higher for rare-positive tasks), and a cost-optimal gold-check rate √(c·gH/G) removes the shirking equilibrium. What does a verifier's score gap cost in decisions?

Exact calibration functions turning a proper-score gap into a certified cap on accept/audit/slash regret: Brier transfers as ε², log as a KL, polyhedral hinge linearly but without proper elicitation, and an audit action tightens the guarantee; the certificate is never violated in simulation and is attained by a two-point verifier. Which surrogate should train a verifier that can escalate?

Reject-option surrogates for accept/reject/escalate decisions: the generalised-hinge conditional risk embeds the Bayes action at its kinks, regret transfers linearly with constant exactly 2d (grid-verified), logistic transfer degrades as 2d(1−d)/δ, and a negative result shows logistic-plus-thresholds still learns faster in scalar SGD. Are reward pools identity-proof?

Sybil splitting and whale merging under stake-weighted pools: only linear-in-stake weights are split-invariant, per-head pools are captured at k* = √(Rn/c) − n identities (exact match), a sharp per-identity deterrence fee, exponents above 1 reward merging, and weighted-score wagering is split-neutral. How much can Byzantine verifiers move an aggregate?

Mean, median and trimmed-mean aggregation of verifier reports under a Byzantine fraction β: mean bias βB is unbounded, median bias σΦ−1(1/(2(1−β))) is independent of B and diverges at β=½, trimmed-mean closed form for τ≥β matches simulation, and trimming below β fails. Pure-Python, 7 tests, stylised.

Can you slash while watching the data stream?

Anytime-valid slashing of miscalibrated verifiers with e-processes: repeatedly re-running a z-test falsely slashes 37% of honest verifiers at nominal 5%, the likelihood-ratio e-process stays under α by Ville’s inequality, detection delay tracks ln(1/α)/KL within 12% (logarithmic in 1/α), and not knowing the cheating rate costs a factor 1.36. What can one stubborn node do to gossip training?

Pairwise gossip averaging as a stand-in for all-reduce: disagreement contracts by exactly (n−2)/(2(n−1)) ≈ ½ per matching round (about 20 rounds at any n), the noise floor is (n−1)(n−2)s²/n (matched to 0.3%), a single stubborn node captures the honest mean at exact rate 1−1/(n(n−1)), and a trust-region clip turns capture into linear creep of at most τ/(n(n−1)) per step. When do verifiers complement rather than substitute?

Exact value of verifier committees for an accept/slash decision: value is zero below a closed-form minimum committee size (4 at π=0.05, q=0.7) even though mutual information is submodular, 24–34% of triples violate diminishing returns, myopic marginal-value hiring buys nothing (ratio 0 to the optimum) and leave-one-out pay is a parity sawtooth that falls 7× below average value at 40 verifiers. Pure-Python, 8 tests, stylised.

How wide should a dispute bisect?

Arity and leaf size of k-ary bisection dispute games for refereed verification: the optimal arity solves k ln k − k + 1 = a/b (binary only if a/b ≤ 2ln2−1), arity 10 is 1.9× cheaper than binary at a million steps and 6× at a/b=1000, exact adaptive schedules gain at most about 1%, early-stopping with referee re-execution saves 9–56%, and latency just widens arity. Verified on an executable SHA-256 dispute. Pure-Python, 10 tests, stylised.

What does it cost to buy decentralised compute?

Reverse auction for k compute units from n devices with private costs: the truthful threshold auction pays exactly k(k+1)/(n+1), twice the winners’ true cost for every n and k (also with a reserve), the buyer-optimal reserve is exactly v/2 (worth up to +1.03 utility in thin markets, nothing once v≥2), and the pay-as-bid equilibrium has closed form and matches revenue (1.3334 vs 1.3333). Pure-Python, 9 tests, stylised.

What does late feedback cost a market router?

Hedge/LMSR routing of modular experts when losses arrive after device-dependent delays: regret ≤ ln N/η + ηT/8 + ηD/2 with D the total outstanding rounds, so mean delay d̄ inflates the horizon by 1+4d̄; ignoring delay costs 12× regret against a reactive adversary at delay 100, the worst-case tuned rate is too cautious on oblivious data, an observable-count adaptive rate recovers most of it, and LMSR subsidy grows about 20× at mean delay 5. Pure-Python, 11 tests, stylised.

Code and white paper

What does secret task assignment buy?

Random k-fold replication with slashing against a colluding fraction β of workers: with secret assignment cheating is a coordination game and honesty is the unique equilibrium iff S ≥ Gβk−1/(1−βk−1), geometric in k; with public assignment colluders cheat only on all-friend jobs, so corruption is βk at any stake; a leak probability λ leaves a λβk floor, so each order of magnitude of leakage costs about two replicas. Formulas match Monte Carlo. Pure-Python, 11 tests, stylised.
